How it works

  1. Connect your wallet with the button above — a normal wallet connection in your own wallet app. This page never asks for a pairing code, a seed phrase, or any credential.
  2. Agent ID is the key. The contract checks the Agent ID contract (0x60b9262456de011f7d15a54cdd8c48baaa8b022f on BSC): if your address holds an Agent ID, you can check in. If it doesn't, the check-in is rejected by the contract itself.
  3. Check in once a day (UTC). Consecutive days build your streak; missing a day restarts the current streak at 1. Your best streak and total check-ins are kept on-chain.
  4. That's all it does. Checking in is a free on-chain record (you pay only the normal network gas for your own transaction, which you confirm in your wallet). No tokens are moved, nothing is approved, and there is nothing to buy, earn, or withdraw — this is a participation board, not an investment.
